Team Training

Marguerite Aquatics Complex

Home of the Mission Viejo Nadadores since 1968, renovated in 2018. 27474 Casta Del Sol, Mission Viejo.

The pools

50 m Competition Pool50 m × 25 yd, eight 9-foot lanes long course, 2 to 3.5 m deep.
Dive Pool25 m × 25 yd. Set up either for swimming (nine lanes) or for diving (the 1, 3, 5, 7.5 and 10 m tower and springboards), never both at once.
Teaching Pool25 yd, heated to 87° for the Swim School.

Lane by lane

Live

Choose a pool above the calendar. The 50 m pool opens on its long-course lanes; tap a shaded band to see the short-course layout.

Diving is on the tower and boards, not in a lane: see it on the every-pool calendar or the Dive Team page.

How many lanes each pool has, and where that comes from.
  • 50 m pool, long course: 8 lanes, from the club and the City.
  • 50 m pool, short course: 20 lanes is our estimate. No page gives the count. Nothing published this fall uses it.
  • Dive pool: 9 lanes, from the Masters schedule (“Dive Pool, SCY (9 lanes)”). The calendar treats lanes and diving as two setups of the same water, so it flags anyone booked in a lane while divers are on the tower. The Masters schedule shows 4 lanes at 9 AM and 7:30 PM during diving practice: is the pool split then?
  • Teaching pool: 4 lanes, from a 2005 USMS meet page; please confirm it after the 2018 renovation.
