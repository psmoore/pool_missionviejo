Every pool at the Marguerite Aquatics Complex on one page: the 50 m pool, the dive pool and the teaching pool, with Masters, diving and the Swim School side by side. Use the arrows for another day, or the filter row for one group.

One schedule for the whole complex. Each program's page below shows the same calendar, filtered to that program.

Four websites, one pool deck. The club's schedules live in four places today, and none of them shows the pools together:
  • Swim Team on SportsEngine (mvnswim.org), where practice times sit behind the member sign-in.
  • Masters on a second SportsEngine site, with a typed workout schedule.
  • Diving on mvndiving.com, one page per group.
  • Swim School on mvnswimschool.org, with 184 classes in Jackrabbit.
Here they are one calendar. A coach who moves a practice moves it once, and every page that shows it, and every family that follows that group, sees the change.
