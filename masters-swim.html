Masters Program

Adult swimming for health, fitness and exercise. Two free workouts before you commit.

Workout week

Live

Every coached workout, with the pool and the number of lanes. Summer/Fall schedule, from August 3.

The schedule gives lane counts, which is more than most. On the calendar:
  • 28 workouts a week: five each weekday, two on Saturday, one on Sunday. The team page promises 30+ coached workouts.
  • The 9:00 AM and 7:30 PM dive-pool workouts have 4 lanes; which four is our estimate.
  • No workouts are shown during the Turkey Classic (November 20–23) or in the dive pool during the Chris Vonk dive meet (December 4–6).

Swim with Orange County's premier Masters team

One-hour workouts with an optional structured set, supervised by the elite coaching staff. Parents of competitive swimmers and college students are eligible for discounts.
