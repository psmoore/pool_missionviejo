Live

The competition pool lane by lane. Swim Team practices appear here as soon as the club adds them.

The one schedule not published anywhere public. Every other Nadadores program posts its times. The Swim Team's practice calendar is on SportsEngine behind the member sign-in, so the 50 m pool shows only Masters (weekdays at 5 AM and noon, Sundays at 8:30 AM) and stands empty the rest of the day. For the pool's real day, we need from the club:
  • Each group's practice days and times: Age Group Gold, Silver and Bronze; Senior Gold, Silver and Bronze; Elite.
  • Which pool and how many lanes each group uses, and when the 50 m pool switches to short course.
The Turkey Classic (November 20–23) is already in, across all three pools.

Divisions

Age Group

Gold, Silver and Bronze. Swimmers learning to turn fundamental swimming skills into racing skills, competing in USA Swimming meets.

Senior

Gold (8 swim practices a week plus 3 dryland), Silver (7 plus 3) and Bronze (6 plus dryland), from high school swimmers to Junior National qualifiers.

Elite

Professional and national-team swimmers training for the Olympic Trials, World Championships and Nationals.
