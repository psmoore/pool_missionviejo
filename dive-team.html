Dive Team

An affiliate of USA Diving, training on the 1, 3, 5, 7.5 and 10 m tower at Marguerite.

Every group's practices for the week, 2026–2027 regular season. Tap a group in the filter row to see only its times.

Fifteen groups, spread across the dive site's program pages, now on one calendar. Each program page posts its own groups' times. Put together:

Dive programs

Elite Team

Elite Morning, Elite 1–4, Elite Blue and Junior Elite 1–3: USA Diving national champions and national-team athletes.

Novice

Novice 2a, 2b, 3a and 3b, named for practices per week. Every competitive diver starts here.

Masters Diving

Adults 21 and over, recreational to competitive, on every board and platform.

Gym to Dive

For current and former gymnasts, Saturdays 5:00–7:00 PM.
