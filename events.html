What a meet does to the pool

Live

The every-pool calendar. Step to November 20 to see the Turkey Classic take all three pools.

A meet changes every program's week, and each program finds out separately. On the calendar, the Turkey Classic replaces Masters, diving, lessons and Junior Team practices in all three pools for four days. That is our assumption: the club's pages do not say which practices a home meet cancels, and the Swim School's session runs straight through the meet.
