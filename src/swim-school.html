The lesson week

Live

Every group lesson in the Fall 2026 session (October 1 to November 30), with Junior Teams and the Fall League. Tap a level in the filter row to see only its classes.

Each block is a half-hour lesson time; open one to see which levels meet then. Private lessons are not shown.

The class list knew more than the website. The levels are described on the website; the times are only in the registration system. Read together:
  • 130 group classes a week in 48 half-hour slots: weekdays 3:00–6:30 PM, weekends 9:00 AM–1:00 PM.
  • Adult lessons: the website says Mondays; the class list also runs them Mondays and Wednesdays, 7:15–8:00 PM.
  • Junior Teams and the Fall League publish times but not which pool. They are shown in short-course lanes 1–3 of the 50 m pool, our estimate.
